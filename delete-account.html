Scriptum

Delete your account

This page lets you permanently delete your Scriptum account without opening the app — for example if you've already uninstalled it. You can also delete your account inside the app: Profile → Delete account.

What gets deleted

  • Your account record — the email is irreversibly rewritten to a generated placeholder and can no longer be used to sign in.
  • All sign-in sessions and refresh tokens.
  • Your streaks and activity history.
  • Any subscription entitlements attached to the account (Google Play keeps its own purchase record).

Reading progress, bookmarks and the downloaded Bible live only on your device — uninstalling the app removes them.

Signed up with Google? Those accounts have no password. Open the app and use Profile → Delete account, set a password first in Profile → Set password and return here, or email us at codezaur@gmail.com from the account's email address and we'll process the deletion.
